What it does

Everything between “we are out of milk” and dinner on the table

Six small things that add up to fewer forgotten items, less food in the bin and a shorter time in the shop.

Shared in real time

Anyone at home adds an item and it shows up on every phone within a second, with their name next to it.

Weekly meal plan

Drag recipes onto the days of the week. The ingredients you do not already have land on the list.

Sorted by aisle

The list reorders itself to match the layout of your usual shop, so you walk it once from door to till.

Pantry memory

Larder remembers what you bought and roughly how long it lasts, and suggests the staples before they run out.

Use-it-first reminders

A nudge on Wednesday about the spinach from Saturday, with two recipes that would finish it.

Budget at a glance

Snap the receipt and see what the week cost, by category, next to the four weeks before it.

How it works

Three habits, about five minutes a week

Step one

Everyone adds as they notice

The last of the coffee, the school bake sale, the thing for the neighbour. Say it, type it or scan the barcode on the empty packet.

  • Voice entry from the lock screen
  • Duplicates merge on their own
  • Notes and photos for the tricky items

Plan the week on Sunday

Step three

Shop once, in order

Whoever is nearest the shop opens the list. Items are grouped by aisle and tick off for everyone as they go into the trolley, so nobody buys the same thing twice.
