    02

    Open source · On-prem

    Runs inside your walls

    Slidr runs on the desktop, with no accounts and no cloud of its own. Connect it to the models your organisation approved, and every deck, prompt and photo stays inside your network.

    • Your models, your gateway

      The agent works with approved models: Azure, AWS, Google Cloud or your own server.

    • Nothing leaves the network

      No telemetry and no sign-in. Files and the agent’s work stay on the machine.

    • Managed in-house, for everyone

      A signed library and updates from an internal server, and central control of agents, models and languages.

    • Open source you can audit

      Licensed under AGPL-3.0: read every line and build it yourself.
