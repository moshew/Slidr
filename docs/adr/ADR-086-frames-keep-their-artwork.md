# ADR-086 — A frame that is made larger keeps its artwork: the photograph takes the change

Status: Implemented in the 2026-10-09 working tree; **not committed** at the time of this record. This changes how the decorated photo frames of [ADR-075](ADR-075-elements-and-presets.md) and the event magnets of [ADR-083](ADR-083-event-magnets.md) are drawn and sized.

## Context

A decorated frame (an instant photo, a phone, a picture frame, a magnet) was a picture of one size: its artwork was laid out in the frame's own box and stretched with the element. Made larger, the card around the photograph grew thicker and its tape, its corners and its stickers grew with it; stretched by one side, they were distorted. The user asked that the graphic elements of a frame keep their thickness, width and height when the frame is made larger, and that only the area of the photograph grow.

## Decision in the working tree

- **The artwork of a frame keeps its size.** `smartFrame` has a new optional `scale`: how many slide pixels a unit of the artwork is. In a picture that is larger than its artwork, or of other proportions, the margins between the edges of the artwork and its opening are as wide as they were, and the opening takes up the difference (`frameLayout` in `packages/model/src/imageFrame.ts`). Only a picture that is too small for its artwork has it drawn smaller, whole, so an opening is never narrower or lower than the one the artwork was drawn around, and nothing is ever squeezed. `imageOpening`, crop mode and the renderer all read that one layout.
- **Artwork that says no scale is a picture of one size, stretched with its element**, opening and all, as every frame was before: the same layout says so. A deck that was saved with the earlier frames is drawn as it was, and what stands beside such a picture in a magnet is stretched with it as in any group.
- **The artwork lays itself out.** It is no longer paths of one size. Each decorated frame has one `drawing`: an SVG with no `viewBox`, in which a unit is a unit of the box it is drawn in, and every part says where it stands in a box of any size, with percentages and CSS `calc()`: so far from the start of the box, from its end, from its middle, or at its place along the opening. Nothing is computed when a frame is resized; the browser lays the SVG out again, as it does while a handle is dragged. `scripts/frames-art.mjs` is the vocabulary (`sheet`): a frame is still drawn in the coordinates of its own box, and each coordinate is tied to the box. `FrameArt.layers` and the path helpers that only it used are gone.
- **What repeats, repeats.** The perforations of the film frame are a pattern laid from the middle of the strip, so a longer strip has more holes of the same size; the stamp's bites and the equaliser of the party magnet likewise. The scalloped window of the baby magnet is as wide as a whole number of scallops (CSS `round()`, with what the card leaves it as the fallback), so a scallop stands on every corner at any size. The arch of the wedding magnet is half a circle as wide as the arch is.
- **The card lies over the photograph's corners, so the photograph is not cut** where that is enough (cards, devices, magnets). The frames that leave the photograph's edge bare still cut it: an ellipse, the arch of `arch-line`, and round corners (`FrameArt.round`), which are in the artwork's units and drawn at its scale.
- **A frame casts its shadow as a whole**, the photograph included, as the magnets already did: `effects.shadow` of the picture, or of the group. A shadow on a card with a window also fell inward, on the photograph. Putting a picture in a frame gives it the frame's shadow and takes away the shadow of the frame it was in; a bare picture keeps its own in a frame that casts none.
- **Beside the picture of a magnet, stickers and captions keep their size too** (`stage/groups.ts`). In a group that a frame made, the picture alone takes a stretch; what is beside it goes with the third of the picture it is at (the first third keeps its distance from that side, the last from the other, the middle stays between), and the box of a caption reaches as far as its two ends go, in type of the same size. Only where the artwork itself is drawn smaller are they made smaller with it. A group that no frame made is stretched as before.
- **An edge handle of a framed picture moves alone**, as the edge of a bare picture does; a corner still sizes it in its proportions. Before, every handle scaled the whole frame.
- **A frame of the earlier catalogue takes the artwork its frame has now when it is resized** (`freshFrame` in `frames.ts`, called by every resize: a handle, an arrow key, a number field, several elements together). It happens in the resize's own step, so one undo brings back the picture as it was. The new artwork is drawn as large as fits the box the picture has, so a frame that was only made larger looks as it did, and one that was stretched is no longer distorted; the stickers of a magnet, which a stretch of the group had stretched too, are again as large as the frame draws them, where they stand. The frame is told by the name of the picture or of its group, or, for a picture of the user's own that was put into a frame, by artwork whose box and opening only one frame has. The frames are loaded when the first such resize begins.
- **A frame is added with its artwork as large as its box is to the frame's own** (a magnet at 1.2267, as large as the safe margins allow), and keeps that size from then on.
- **Thumbnails of every decorated frame are drawn by the renderer** (`DrawnFrame`), as the magnets' were, so the panel shows what lands on the slide.

## Considered and rejected

- **Stretching the artwork in nine slices** (corners as they are, sides along one axis) distorts whatever stands on a side: tape, perforations, confetti, a round ring.
- **Mapping the points of the old paths** when the element is drawn needs a rule for every outline (a round corner must not stretch, a ring must stay round, a row of holes must repeat), and cannot touch the drawings of the magnets, which are SVG.
- **Drawing the artwork anew on every resize** in the app would have to be done by every way an element is sized (the handles, the number fields, a group, the agent), and the exported file could not do it at all.

## Verification

- Unit tests: `imageFrame.test.ts` (the layout at larger, smaller and other proportions, and where a part of the artwork lies), `SlideRenderer.test.tsx`, `crop.test.ts`, `frames.test.ts` (every decorated frame has a drawing with no `viewBox`; scale when added; the shadow in and out of frames; a frame of the earlier catalogue told by name and by its artwork, alone and in a magnet) and `space.test.ts` (a frame's group resized larger and smaller, a plain group and a group around stretched artwork as before). The 895 unit tests of the model, the renderer, the export and the app areas touched pass.
- E2E on a dev server of its own: `elements-panel.spec.ts` with two new tests (an edge and a corner of an instant photo, a frame narrower than its card, and a magnet whose sticker and caption keep their size; a frame of the earlier catalogue that takes its new artwork when an edge is dragged, and gives it back on undo), `stage-crop-frame.spec.ts`, `media-replace.spec.ts`, `stage-image-handles.spec.ts` and `numeric-placement.spec.ts`: 44 tests pass. No other E2E file was run.
- All 42 decorated frames were rendered around a photograph at their own size, enlarged evenly, widened, heightened and at half size, the magnets in Hebrew and in English, and looked at; the panel's thumbnails in the light and the dark interface. In the app a polaroid and a magnet were added, filled and resized by their handles. A slide with a resized magnet, stamp and phone was exported to HTML and the file drawn beside the Stage: the two are the same.
- A magnet as the earlier catalogue made it and an old stretch left it (the user's own, rebuilt from the catalogue of before), an old instant photo and an old phone around a picture with a name of its own were put on a slide of the app: each is drawn as it was, takes its new artwork at the first drag of a handle, and comes back whole on undo.

## Open for the user

- **A frame of the earlier catalogue is not changed until it is resized**: a deck opens as it was saved, stretched artwork and all. A picture of the user's own in a wooden or a gold frame, or in one of the two offset frames (`block-behind`, `outline-behind`), cannot be told from its twin by its artwork, and one in the browser window has an opening the catalogue no longer has: these stay as they are, and clicking the frame again in the panel gives them the new artwork.
- A frame cannot be made narrower than its artwork without the artwork being drawn smaller: there is no size at which a border is squeezed. A magnet that is narrowed therefore becomes smaller as a whole.
- The scallops and the perforations use CSS `round()` and geometry properties in SVG (Chromium 125, Firefox 118, Safari 15.4 and later). In an older browser the exported file still shows the frame; only the scalloped window is not fitted to whole scallops.
- A caption keeps its type size when a magnet is made larger, so on a much larger magnet the words are small beside the photograph: the type is sized by hand, as any text is.
