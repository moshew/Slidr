# ADR-083 — Event magnets: photo frames that come with stickers and a caption

Status: Implemented in the 2026-10-09 working tree; **not committed** at the time of this record. This extends the photo frames of the Elements browser in [ADR-075](ADR-075-elements-and-presets.md).

## Context

The user asked for a group of frames like a reference picture: the frame of a magnet that a photographer hands out at an event. Such a frame is a printed card with a window for the photograph, a few stickers over its corners and the name of the event. The frames that existed were a picture alone: an outline that cuts the photograph, or artwork of flat paths around an opening.

## Decision in the working tree

- **A new group, `magnets` ("מגנטים לאירועים" / "Event magnets"), with ten frames**, after the picture frames: a team day (`magnet-summer`, the reference's own subject), a birthday, a wedding, a bar or bat mitzvah, a new baby, the end of a school year, a company event, a party at night, a trip and a children's party. Eight are 1050×750 and two (`magnet-wedding`, `magnet-party`) are 750×1050. Each has a structure of its own: two pastels and polka dots, confetti and a ribbon, an arch with lines of gold, night blue with sparks and a medallion, a window with a scalloped edge, a blackboard in a wooden frame, the deck's theme colours, tubes of neon over an equaliser, a map with a trail, a rainbow behind the photograph.
- **On a slide a magnet is a group: the picture, and beside it elements of their own.** The card is one SVG (`art.drawing`), a decoration of the picture's `smartFrame` like the artwork of the other frames. The stickers are `svg` elements and the captions are `text` elements, children of the group beside the picture. The alternative, everything inside `smartFrame.decorations`, was rejected: decorations take no pointer, so the name of the event could not be typed over, and a sticker that hides a face could not be moved or deleted. `frames.ts` holds this as `extras` of a frame; a frame without extras is the picture alone, as before.
- **Stickers are drawings of Fluent Emoji (MIT)**, copied by the generator into the catalogue once (`stickers`, by name), with the set's notice beside the catalogue (`frames-catalog.notice.json`), which the build's notices pick up. Three are composed of them (a sun in sunglasses, three balloons and two crayons, recoloured by a hue filter), and the Star of David medallion is drawn. `scripts/frames-magnets.mjs` draws the group; `generate-frames-catalog.mjs` calls it. The catalogue grew from 276 kB to 763 kB; it is still loaded only when frames are first shown.
- **A magnet comes as large as the slide's safe margins allow** (1288×920, or 657×920), not at its catalogue size. When a group is made larger by hand its text keeps its size, so a magnet that came small would need its caption resized; made smaller, the captions follow (`autoFit: 'shrink'`). Captions are scaled with the box when a magnet is added or put around a picture. A caption is one line at a line height of 1.15, and the generator refuses a caption whose box is lower than its line or a sticker that reaches outside the card, so the group is exactly as large as the card.
- **The shadow is the group's**, not a layer's: a shadow on a layer with a window also falls inward, on the photograph.
- **Putting a selected picture in a frame (`reframe`)** now has four cases. Between two plain frames it is the patch it was. With a magnet on either side, the picture (or the group an earlier magnet made around it) is replaced in place by one `element.replace`: by a group of the new magnet, or by the picture alone, which takes the group's place and rotation. What the earlier magnet put beside the picture goes; elements the user added to the group stay; a caption that was typed over keeps its words in the new magnet, and one that was not gives way to the new magnet's own.
- **In the panel**, a frame with a drawing is drawn small by the renderer itself (`DrawnFrame`: the frame alone on a slide, around the stand-in landscape), in the deck's language and theme, so the thumbnail is what lands on the slide. The group is shown two in a row. A selected group that a frame made counts as its picture (`useSelectedPicture`, through `useTargetIn` of `objects/target.ts`): the collection offers to fill it, a click on another frame changes its frame, and a picture chosen in the media panel goes into it.
- `magnet-company` is drawn in the theme's `primary`, `accent` and `bg` through the theme's CSS variables, and its captions are in the deck's own font: it follows the template.

## Verification

- `frames.test.ts`: the ten magnets as valid groups in both languages, captions that their boxes hold, the size on a slide, and the four cases of `reframe` through a `CommandBus` with undo. `elements-panel.spec.ts`: one new test (add, fill from the computer, type the caption, change the magnet, back to a plain frame). `media-replace.spec.ts`: one new test (a picture of the deck goes into a selected magnet). The 20 tests of the two files pass on a server of their own; no other E2E file was run.
- Every magnet was rendered in Hebrew and in English at its full size, on a slide of the app around a photograph, and at thumbnail size in the panel (light and dark); every caption was measured against its box (none above 85%), and each was put through the app's design check as it lands on a slide. What the check still reports: L11 (colours and fonts that are not the template's, as the ready-made designs do), and for the two upright magnets L07, since one upright frame cannot cover 60% of a slide.
- A slide with a magnet was exported to HTML and the file was opened: card, stickers, caption and shadow are drawn as on the slide.

## Open for the user

- The sample captions and names (Noa, Maya and Jonathan, Itamar, Mika) are placeholders for the user's own words; no caption carries a year.
- The thumbnails show the stand-in landscape of all the frames, not a photograph of each event.
- Holidays (Purim, Hanukkah, Rosh Hashanah) were left out of the ten as not generic; they would be further entries of the same kind in `frames-magnets.mjs`.
