# Slidr design quality instruction

When creating or changing ready-made slide designs, invitations, marketing slides, video thumbnails, or examples in Elements:

- Produce work that looks professionally art directed and publication ready. A flat background, a heading, and a few basic shapes are a wireframe. Give every design a clear concept, relevant imagery or crafted illustration, refined typography, purposeful detail, and a strong focal point.
- Make each example visually distinct for its purpose. Changing only colors or swapping a title does not count as a different design.
- When a visual reference is supplied, compare its subject scale, typography, contrast, decorative density, layering and sense of movement with the rendered result. Match the level of craft; do not add arbitrary ornaments that cross text, faces or key product details.
- Use original, meaningful sample text. Do not ship placeholder text, dummy image frames, lorem ipsum, or stale event dates. Keep text and useful framing elements editable. Bundle image assets with the presentation so the design survives saving and works offline.
- Render every design at full slide size and thumbnail size in Hebrew and English. Inspect the actual rendered pixels. Fix text overflow, awkward wraps, clipping, weak contrast, poor photo crops, uneven alignment, and RTL mistakes, then render again.
- Do not call the work done merely because schema validation or automated tests pass. Include a visual review of every design and correct what that review reveals.
